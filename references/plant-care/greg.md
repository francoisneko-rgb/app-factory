## Voix des utilisateurs (avis App Store, 10 avis riches — mostly helpful)
**Ce qui fait rêver (5★)**
- **Algorithme qui apprend** : "The algorithm updates as you water more frequently or less
  frequently. That's really nice."
- **Personnalisation** : "You get to name your plants!", "lets you set lots of criteria…

- **Communauté/social** : "I love the social aspect of the app", "see what other people are

- **Simplicité/design** : "clean look and simplicity".

**Plaintes / frictions**
1. **Paywall « Super Greg » massif** (le feature n°1 plaint) : "any features like
   notifications for water and fertilizing reminders **only come with premium, which is a
   weekly subscription**", "literally cannot do anything else on this app without premium",
   "I don't understand why I have to subscribe to super greg in order to track my plants
   watering".
2. **Algorithme d'arrosage pas fiable partout** : "watering schedule is totally unreliable
   and random", "doesn't correctly account for factors", "I live at high altitude and my
   plants dry out much faster".
3. **Pas de création de son propre planning** : "wish we could program the watering schedule
   ourselves".
4. **Free trial → abo accidentel** : "used this free trial for about 2 days and then forgot
   to cancel… got charged", "Apple won't refund".
5. **Identification limitée** : "Plant ID feature is lacking", "Bush on Fire Croton was ID'd
   as a Gold Dust Croton".
6. **Bugs/profils** : "names of my plants kept resetting or copying", "profiles would either
   clone themselves, or disappear entirely", "freezes".
7. **Navigation trop de taps** : "click click click to get to where I need".

## Forces / Faiblesses
- **Forces** : communauté sociale unique, algorithme qui apprend, personnalisation poussée,
  nommage des plantes, design clean.
- **Faiblesses** : **paywall excessif** (tout derrière Super Greg, abo hebdo), algorithme
  d'arrosage non fiable en climats spécifiques, pas de planning manuel, identification
  limitée, bugs, free trial piégeux.
