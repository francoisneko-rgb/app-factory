**Plaintes / frictions**
1. **Timing trompeur** : "lists the workout for 12 minutes, but it takes 18 minutes because

2. **Pas de progression avec l'utilisateur** : "The workouts regenerate every 28 days and
   you restart at a beginners level, so the app does not progress with you."
3. **Exercices qui se répètent** : "specific exercises have begun to repeat themselves. I
   got the same routine three times in a row."
4. **Pas de mode hors-ligne** : "I would like to see the ability to use the app offline…
   traveling".
5. **Pas de cast/TV ni multi-appareil** : "no way to download to another device or cast it
   to a tv".
6. **Voix off parfois incohérente avec la vidéo** : "verbal instructions say to touch your
   ankle" alors que l'avatar touche le genou.
7. **Pas de reset de mot de passe**.
8. **Pas de revoir l'exercice après coup** : "once you do the exercise you can't see it after
   the first time you do it".
